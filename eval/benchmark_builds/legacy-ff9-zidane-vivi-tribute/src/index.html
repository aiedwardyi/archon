Character Biography

The Tail-Bearing Thief from Tantalus

Zidane Tribal is the protagonist of Final Fantasy IX. Unlike the brooding heroes of earlier installments, Zidane approaches the world with irreverent charm and a genuine care for those around him. As a member of Tantalus Theatre Troupe — a group of actors doubling as thieves — he stumbles into the greatest adventure of his life.

Born on Terra and sent to Gaia as part of Kuja's grand design, Zidane grapples with questions of identity and purpose. His philosophy is simple: "You don't need a reason to help people." It's this selfless worldview that makes him one of gaming's most beloved protagonists.

Biography

The Black Mage Who Asked "What Am I?"

Vivi Ornitier is a young black mage who joins Zidane's party after a chance encounter in Alexandria. With his oversized hat, glowing yellow eyes, and tiny trembling frame, Vivi quickly became one of gaming's most beloved characters — an unlikely hero grappling with the deepest of existential questions.

Discovering that he is a manufactured weapon — one of thousands of Black Mage soldiers created by Kuja — Vivi must confront questions of life, death, identity, and what it means to exist. Yet through his journey, he finds friendship, courage, and a reason to live that transcends his origins.

In battle, Vivi is a powerhouse of elemental destruction. His Black Magic repertoire spans every element — Fire, Blizzard, Thunder, Water, Flare, and Doomsday — making him the party's premier offensive spellcaster. His Trance form, Double Black, allows him to cast two spells simultaneously at devastating effect.

Story Arc

A Journey of Becoming

Act I — Alexandria
An Accidental Hero

A shy black mage traveling alone to see his first play in Alexandria. A chance encounter with Zidane sets him on a path far beyond anything he imagined.

Act II — Black Mage Village
The Truth of His Existence

Discovering a village of fellow Black Mages — manufactured soldiers fighting for Kuja — Vivi confronts the horrifying truth of what he is and how long he has left to live.

Act III — Terra
The Weight of a Soul

On the dying world of Terra, Vivi discovers the full scope of Kuja's plan and his own place in it. With his friends by his side, he chooses to fight — not for revenge, but for the time he has.

Epilogue — A Letter Never Forgotten
"I'm Glad I Was Born"

Vivi's final letter, read in the game's epilogue, stands as one of the most poignant farewells in gaming history. A meditation on mortality, love, and what it means to have truly lived.

Story Chapters

The Road to Crystal World

Chapter I
Alexandria — A Fateful Performance

Tantalus attempts to kidnap Princess Garnet during a theatrical performance. The plan goes sideways when Garnet herself wants to be kidnapped, and a masked black mage named Vivi wanders into the chaos.

Chapter II
Lindblum & the Mist Continent

The party escapes to Lindblum where they meet Regent Cid and learn of the threat posed by Alexandria. The Festival of the Hunt and Vivi's growing magical power mark the beginning of his true journey.

Chapter III
Terra & the Iifa Tree

The truth of both Zidane and Vivi's origins are revealed. Kuja's plan to use the Iifa Tree to destroy Gaia comes to light, and the party races to stop him before all life on the planet is extinguished.

Final Chapter
Crystal World — The Final Stand

Necron emerges as the ultimate threat to existence itself. Zidane leads his friends into the heart of nothingness — driven by love, courage, and the unshakeable belief that life is worth living and fighting for.
