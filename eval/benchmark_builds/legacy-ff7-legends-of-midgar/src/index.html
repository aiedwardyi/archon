The Lifestream

The Lifestream

The spiritual energy of the Planet — a flowing river of life essence that connects all living things. Shinra drains it using Mako reactors to power their empire.

Materia

Crystallized Lifestream energy that grants magical abilities. Found in the Planet's veins, materia allows wielders to cast spells, summon monsters, and bend reality.

Jenova

An ancient extraterrestrial calamity that fell from the sky 2,000 years ago. Its cells were used to create SOLDIER, and its influence corrupts Cloud's very memories.

The Promised Land

A mythical destination promised by the Cetra — the Ancients who could commune with the Planet. Shinra believes it holds limitless Mako energy and will stop at nothing to find it.
