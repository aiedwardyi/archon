Evolution Paths

Each starter Pokémon has two evolution stages. Charmander evolves into Charmeleon at level 16, then Charizard at 36. Squirtle becomes Wartortle at 16, then Blastoise at 36. Bulbasaur evolves into Ivysaur at 16, then Venusaur at 32.
