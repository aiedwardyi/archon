The Planet's Cry

The world is poisoned by the Shinra Electric Power Company, a mega-corporation that drains the very life energy of the planet—Mako—to fuel its insatiable greed. As the planet dies, a small resistance group called AVALANCHE rises to fight back.

Led by Barret Wallace and aided by the enigmatic Cloud Strife, AVALANCHE launches a desperate campaign of sabotage against Shinra's Mako reactors. But their fight awakens something far more dangerous—the legendary SOLDIER Sephiroth, thought dead, returns with a plan to become a god.

Caught between Shinra's corporate tyranny and Sephiroth's apocalyptic ambitions, Cloud and his allies must uncover the truth about their pasts, the ancient Cetra, and the Lifestream itself. The fate of the entire planet rests on their shoulders.

The planet's greatest legacy... should not be the world's end.
